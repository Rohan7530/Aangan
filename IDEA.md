# Aangan

The family group chat where everyone reads in their own way.

## Problem
Family group chats are built for the young members.
Elders face small text, the wrong language, and scam forwards.
So they go silent, or they get cheated.

## Idea in one line
One message, delivered to each person in the form they can use.

## The four features
1. Reader view: each member sets font size and language once.
   Every message arrives that way, with a read-aloud button.
2. Voice reply: speak, and the group receives text.
3. Scam shield: risky messages ("send money", unknown links)
   show a clear warning to senior members.
4. Daily check-in: one tap for "I'm fine".
   The family is alerted if it is missed.

## Plus: Judge Mode
A demo room with a bot family, so one judge on one device
sees every feature in under two minutes.

## Who it is for
- Main user: an elder (60+) in a family group.
- Buyer and inviter: the adult child who sets it up.

## Why it grows
One person joins and brings the whole family.

## Track
Humanitarian & Social Good (also fits Social & Community).

## What we will NOT build
Calls, stories, payments, stickers, public groups.
