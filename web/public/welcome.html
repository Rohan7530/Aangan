Scam shield

Scams don't arrive in the family group. So the shield doesn't wait there.

Most frauds come by SMS, calls and WhatsApp from strangers. Dadi can bring any message to Aangan and ask one question. If it is a trap, the shield says so in her language, out loud, and brings her family in before money moves.

This page runs Aangan's rule layer in your browser. In the app, anything the rules flag also gets an AI check.

Layer 1 · inside Aangan

Every incoming message is checked

Fast rules catch OTP and KYC requests, money amounts, look-alike links and "don't tell anyone". Only flagged messages go to the AI, so the shield works even when the AI is down.

Layer 2 · from anywhere

"Is this safe?" for SMS, calls and WhatsApp

Dadi pastes a message, shares a screenshot, or says "someone called saying he is from CBI". She gets a plain answer in her language, read aloud.
